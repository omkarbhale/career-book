Three Games, One You

A field guide to landing the job, being yourself at work, and making the work count

Getting a job, working with people, and being good at the work so that it counts are three different games with different rules. This book teaches all three, one evidence-graded chapter at a time, and argues that the winning move in each is the same: be one real person, and make that person easy to see.
