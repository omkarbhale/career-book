# Part II evidence index

One line per key finding: **grade**, finding, `source key(s)`, and the topic file with details.
Grades: strong / moderate / weak / myth (see `README.md`). Keys resolve in `sources.yaml`.
In the Myths section the grade is the verdict on the *popular claim*, and the keys are the sources that test it.

## First principles (`first-principles.md`)

- **strong**: Reciprocity-based cooperation can start, thrive and resist invasion when players expect to meet again. `axelrod1981`
- **strong**: Cooperation evolves through five mechanisms, including direct and indirect (reputation-based) reciprocity. `nowak2006`, `nowak1998`
- **strong**: Many people pay to punish free-riders, which reduces free-riding. `fehr2000`
- **strong**: Trust rests on perceived ability, benevolence and integrity, which predict trust, performance and citizenship. `mayer1995`, `colquitt2007`
- **weak**: Trust Equation (credibility + reliability + intimacy) / self-orientation is a practitioner heuristic with no published validation. `maister2000`
- **strong**: Bad events and impressions outweigh good ones and form faster. `baumeister2001`
- **moderate**: An unsolicited small favour increased later compliance regardless of liking. `regan1971`
- **moderate**: The pull to reciprocate fades within about a week. `burger1997`
- **strong**: People underestimate by up to 50% how likely others are to agree to direct requests for help. `flynn2008`
- **moderate**: Generosity buys status, balanced exchange tracks productivity, and frequent exchange helps both. `flynn2003`
- **strong**: Helping beyond the role relates to better ratings, rewards and unit performance. `podsakoff2009`
- **moderate**: Extra-role helping is linked to overload, stress and work-family conflict. `bolino2005`, `bergeron2007`
- **strong**: Women are asked more and accept more low-promotability tasks. `babcock2017`
- **strong**: People are judged on two dimensions, warmth and competence, with warmth often first. `fiske2002`, `fiske2007`
- **moderate**: Rudeness, even witnessed, reduces performance and helping. `porath2007`

## Authenticity (`authenticity.md`)

- **strong**: High self-monitors perform better and emerge as leaders more often. `day2002`
- **strong**: The self-monitoring-leadership link mostly disappears in subordinate ratings. `lei2025`
- **moderate**: High self-monitors won more promotions and central network positions. `kilduff1994`, `mehra2001`
- **moderate**: People feel most authentic acting extraverted, agreeable, conscientious and stable, whatever their traits. `fleeson2010`
- **strong**: Felt authenticity correlates with well-being (r = .40) and engagement (r = .37). `sutton2020`
- **strong**: Workplace authenticity research is fragmented; power shapes who can afford authenticity. `cha2019`
- **moderate**: "Authenticity paradox": a rigid self-concept can block growth (practitioner essay). `ibarra2015`
- **strong**: Surface acting relates to impaired well-being; deep acting does not, and it improves customer outcomes. `hulsheger2011`, `grandey2003`
- **moderate**: Racial code-switching is rated more professional, creating pressure to do it. `mccluney2021`
- **moderate**: A higher-status person's disclosure of weakness costs status and influence; a peer's doesn't. `gibson2018`
- **moderate**: Fit between preferred and allowed work-home segmentation predicts stress and satisfaction. `kreiner2006`, `rothbard2005`
- **moderate**: Best-self onboarding improved retention and customer satisfaction. `cable2013`
- **myth**: "Inauthenticity makes people feel immoral" is retracted. `gino2015`

## Formality and communication (`formality.md`)

- **strong**: People overestimate how well their tone comes across in email. `kruger2005`
- **strong**: Receivers overestimate the expected reply speed for after-hours email; a sender's note fixes it. `giurge2021`
- **moderate**: Expectations to monitor email after hours harm employees' and partners' health. `becker2021`, `belkin2020`
- **moderate**: Smileys in work first impressions lower perceived competence and don't raise warmth. `glikson2018`
- **strong**: Successful humour raises status; failed or inappropriate humour lowers it. `bitterly2017`
- **moderate**: The same humour raised men's status but lowered women's in one experiment. `evans2019`
- **moderate**: Positive workplace humour is associated with performance and lower burnout. `mesmermagnus2012`
- **weak**: Profanity lowered impressions of intelligence and trustworthiness in one small vignette study. `defrank2019`
- **moderate**: Deliberate non-conformity raises status only for people already secure in status and norms. `bellezza2014`
- **moderate**: People undervalue the effect of expressing gratitude. `kumar2018`
- **strong**: Trait judgments from faces form within about 100 ms. `willis2006`

## Relationships (`relationships.md`)

- **moderate**: Workplace friendships raise performance (through trust) but also exhaustion: a mixed blessing. `methot2016`
- **moderate**: Friendship and work norms structurally conflict. `pillemer2018`
- **weak**: Promotion is one cause of workplace friendship deterioration. `sias2004`
- **moderate**: Closeness tracks hours together (about 50, 90 and 200+ hours for each stage); work hours count less. `hall2019`
- **strong**: People underestimate how much new acquaintances like them (the liking gap). `boothby2018`, `mastroianni2021`
- **weak**: Gallup says having a "best friend at work" has become more important (commercial claim). `gallup2022`
- **weak**: Asking a favour increases liking (Ben Franklin effect), with thin evidence. `jecker1969`, `niiya2016`
- **moderate**: Most gossip is neutral; prosocial gossip deters exploitation. `robbins2020`, `feinberg2012`
- **moderate**: Coworkers distrust a peer dating a superior and see unfair advantage. `malachowski2012`
- **weak**: 27% of US workers report a past or current workplace romance (SHRM). `shrm2023`
- **strong**: About 15% of US workers reported workplace alcohol use or impairment in a year. `frone2006`
- **strong**: Moderate alcohol increases bonding among strangers. `sayette2012`
- **strong**: Mental detachment off-hours improves well-being without hurting engagement. `sonnentag2012`
- **moderate**: About 1 in 5 employees feel lonely daily; remote workers more (25%). `gallup2024`
- **moderate**: Lonelier employees perform worse, via lower approachability and commitment. `ozcelik2018`

## Teams (`teams.md`)

- **strong**: Team psychological safety predicts learning behaviour, which predicts performance. `edmondson1999`, `frazier2017`
- **moderate**: Psychological safety can also reduce motivation ("slacking off in comfort"). `deng2019`
- **weak**: Google's Project Aristotle ranked psychological safety first (internal, unpublished). `google2015`
- **strong**: Feedback helps on average (d = .41), but over a third of feedback interventions reduce performance. `kluger1996`
- **weak**: The feedback sandwich didn't improve performance. `parkes2013`
- **weak**: Radical Candor is a practitioner model with no peer-reviewed test. `scott2017`
- **strong**: Relationship and process conflict hurt groups; task conflict helps only when relationship conflict is low. `dewit2012`, `dedreu2003`
- **moderate**: Managers penalise challenging voice compared with supportive voice. `burris2012`
- **moderate**: Venting anger increases it. `bushman2002`
- **strong**: Evidence for a general "collective intelligence" factor is weak. `crede2017`

## Power and politics (`power.md`)

- **strong**: Political skill predicts performance and career success beyond personality and IQ. `munyon2015`, `bing2011`, `ferris2005`
- **moderate**: Ingratiation and rational persuasion have positive effects on work outcomes. `higgins2003`
- **strong**: People over-claim credit for joint work. `ross1979`
- **strong**: Credit for group work is biased by gender. `sarsons2021`
- **moderate**: Self-promoters overestimate audience pleasure and come across as braggarts. `scopelliti2015`
- **moderate**: Self-promoting women gain competence ratings but lose likability. `rudman1998`
- **strong**: Leader-member relationship quality differs within teams and drives outcomes. `dulebohn2012`
- **strong**: Mentoring's effects on objective career outcomes are small. `allen2004`, `eby2008`
- **moderate**: Mentoring is not sponsorship (practitioner distinction). `ibarra2010`
- **moderate**: People overestimate the cost of declining invitations. `givi2024`
- **strong**: Group brainstorming produces fewer ideas than individuals working alone. `mullen1991`
- **moderate**: Meeting satisfaction is a distinct part of job satisfaction. `rogelberg2010`

## Remote and hybrid (`remote.md`)

- **strong**: WFH raised call-centre performance 13% and halved attrition, but cut promotions conditional on performance. `bloom2015`
- **strong**: Two WFH days a week cut quits by a third with no effect on performance or promotions. `bloom2024`
- **strong**: Firm-wide remote work made collaboration networks more siloed. `yang2022`
- **strong**: Proximity raises feedback to junior engineers (+18.3%) at a cost to senior output. `emanuel2026`
- **strong**: Telecommuting has small benefits overall; more than 2.5 days a week harms coworker relationships. `gajendran2007`
- **moderate**: Passive face time triggers inferences of dependability and commitment. `elsbach2010`
- **moderate**: Open-plan offices cut face-to-face interaction by about 70%. `bernstein2018`

## Being new (`newcomers.md`)

- **strong**: Role clarity, self-efficacy and social acceptance drive newcomer outcomes. `bauer2007`
- **moderate**: Newcomers who seek information adjust better. `morrison1993`
- **strong**: Feedback-seeking declines with tenure. `anseel2015`
- **weak**: "First 90 days" is a practitioner convention. `watkins2003`

## Mistakes and apologies (`mistakes.md`)

- **strong**: People expect harsher judgment after blunders than they get. `savitsky2001`, `gilovich2000`
- **weak**: The pratfall effect rests on one small 1966 study. `aronson1966`
- **moderate**: More open units detect and report more errors. `edmondson1996`
- **strong**: Apologise for competence failures; integrity violations need different handling. `kim2004`
- **moderate**: More complete apologies work better. `lewicki2016`
- **moderate**: Superfluous apologies increase trust. `brooks2014`

## Burnout (`burnout.md`)

- **strong**: Burn-out is an occupational phenomenon (ICD-11), not a medical condition. `who2019`
- **strong**: Burnout prevalence estimates range from 0 to 80% because definitions vary. `rotenstein2018`
- **strong**: Organisation-directed burnout interventions beat individual ones. `panagioti2017`
- **strong**: Working 55+ hours a week was linked to an estimated 745,000 heart disease and stroke deaths (2016). `pega2021`
- **strong**: Job demands drive strain; resources buffer it (JD-R). `bakker2007`

## Leaving and layoffs (`leaving.md`)

- **strong**: Low pay, no advancement and disrespect were the top reasons for quitting in 2021. `pew2022`
- **strong**: Boomerang managers improve less and leave again more often. `arnold2021`
- **moderate**: Resignation style affects managers' reactions. `klotz2016`
- **strong**: Downsizing raises survivors' voluntary turnover; fair procedures soften it. `trevor2008`
- **strong**: Job displacement raised long-run mortality among high-seniority men. `sullivan2009`
- **strong**: Unemployment lowers psychological and physical well-being. `mckeeryan2005`

## Ethics (`ethics.md`)

- **strong**: Unethical choices come from bad apples, bad cases and bad barrels together. `kishgephart2010`
- **moderate**: Gradual small transgressions lead to larger ones. `welsh2015`
- **strong**: Corporate fraud is mostly detected by employees, media and regulators. `dyck2010`

## Culture (`culture.md`)

- **strong**: About 80% of cultural-value variation is within countries. `taras2016`
- **strong**: Hofstede's values predict work outcomes modestly (ρ = .18). `taras2010`
- **strong**: Cultures vary in tightness, meaning the strength of norms. `gelfand2011`
- **weak**: Culture Map's eight scales are a practitioner framework. `meyer2014`

- **myth**: Power poses change hormones and behaviour. `ranehill2015`, `simmons2017`, `korner2022`, `carney2016`
- **weak**: "People leave managers, not companies" (Gallup); pay and advancement rank as high. `gallup2015`, `pew2022`
- **myth**: 10,000 hours: deliberate practice explains under 1% of performance in professions. `macnamara2014`, `macnamara2019`
- **myth**: The critical positivity ratio (2.9:1). `brown2013`
- **myth**: Dunbar's number (150) as a fixed limit. `lindenfors2021`
- **myth**: Ego depletion (willpower tank). `hagger2016`
- **myth**: The Hawthorne illumination results. `levitt2011`
- **myth**: "EQ matters more than IQ". `joseph2010`, `oboyle2011`
- **myth**: Large generational differences at work. `costanza2012`
